# Master Concept Bank — Sanyam

## 1. Purpose
This Master Concept Bank serves as a centralized, curated repository of concepts, principles, definitions, frameworks, metaphors, scientific ideas, psychological insights, quotes, warnings, practical techniques, data points, experiments, and mini-examples related to the topic of **Sanyam**. It is designed to act as the raw material for building speeches, presentations, and educational content, completely separate from the Prasang (Story) Bank.

## 2. Core Thesis
* Sanyam is **not suppression**; it is **re-orientation**.
* Suppression means fighting desires with pressure. Sanyam means redirecting energy with awareness.
* Modern youth are not only distracted; they are disoriented.
* Sanyam is the **steering wheel** that gives direction to freedom.
* The ecosystem of Sabha, Bhajan, Swadhyay, Jagrata, and Guru-bhav keeps that steering wheel stable.

## 3. Target Sanyam Angles
1. **Attention Sanyam:** Controlling focus in the digital age.
2. **Desire Sanyam:** Managing impulses and sensory inputs.
3. **Aahar Sanyam:** Dietary and sensory consumption filtering.
4. **Reaction / Vani Sanyam:** Emotional regulation and speech control.
5. **Ego / Anti-flex Sanyam:** Humility, simplicity, and avoiding spiritual/material pride.
6. **Time & Focus Sanyam:** Protecting time and prioritizing deep work.
7. **Professional Sanyam:** Ethics, resilience, and detachment in career pursuits.
8. **Reset Sanyam:** Bouncing back from failures and lapses instantly.
9. **Sabha Ecosystem Sanyam:** Utilizing the spiritual environment and peers to maintain discipline.
