Project: Sanyam Presentation

Audience:
- Youth
- Young professionals
- People new to Sabha
- Spiritual learners

Purpose:
To explain Sanyam from all angles: spiritual, practical, youth-life, digital life, professional life, and Sabha life.

Core thesis:
Sanyam is not suppression, not passive patience, and not religious restriction.
Sanyam is active self-mastery.
It is the steering wheel of life, not the brake.
Modern youth are not merely distracted; they are disoriented.
Sanyam, supported by Sabha, Bhajan, Swadhyay, Jagrata, Guru-bhav, and practical boundaries, becomes the compass that restores direction.

Important distinctions:
- Sanyam is not only Dheeraj/patience.
- Sanyam is not forceful suppression.
- Sanyam is not guilt.
- Sanyam is not becoming boring or inactive.
- Sanyam is disciplined freedom.

Key metaphors:
1. Steering wheel, not brake
2. Spring vs river
3. Distraction vs disorientation
4. Empty mind is devil’s workshop
5. Beautiful surface needs terrible depth
6. Sabha melts the spring
7. Sandwich Technique: Dhun/Bhajan before and after important tasks
